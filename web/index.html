How it works

Plug in, drag, play

Connect your unit

Plug your POD Go, Helix or HX Effects into your computer over USB, close POD Go Edit or HX Edit, and click Connect your device.

MORF-X finds the amp

It reads the preset that's loaded, picks the amp, and gives you five tones for it: one in the centre and one in each corner.

Drag the crosshair

Every parameter of the block blends between the tones as you move. A short glide turns a quick move into a smooth sweep.

Save when you like it

Nothing is saved until you say. Save to the preset, or Save as into any setlist and slot.

What's inside

Built for playing, not menu-diving

Five tones per model

Every amp and most effects start with five ready-made tones, Line 6's own Focus View presets.

Make them yours

Rename the tones, dial a corner in on the unit and copy it in, and keep your own set in a file.

Every knob at once

The block's knobs move live on screen, laid out like the unit's own pages, five to a row.

Follows your unit

Change preset or setlist, or stamp an effect on or off, and MORF-X keeps up.

Setlists and Save as

Pick any setlist and preset from the app, and save into any slot you choose.

Safe by design

It edits the loaded preset the way the unit's own editor does. Reload the preset and you're back where you started.

Good to know

Questions

Will it change my presets?

Only when you press Save or Save as. Until then MORF-X edits the loaded preset the way the unit's own editor does, and reloading the preset on the unit puts everything back. Back up your presets in POD Go Edit or HX Edit first anyway: it's always a good habit.

Why won't it connect?

Only one program can talk to the unit at a time. Close POD Go Edit or HX Edit, and any other tab or app using MORF-X, then try again. In the browser, use Chrome or Edge.

Does it need special firmware?

No. MORF-X works with Line 6's own firmware over USB. It also works alongside NAM A2 Lite for POD Go.

Does it work on a phone or iPad?

Not yet: phone and tablet browsers can't talk to USB devices. Use a computer with Chrome or Edge, or the app.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. MORF-X is free and open source (GPL-3.0), made by ToneShift.
