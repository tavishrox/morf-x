MORF-X device check

This shows which Line 6 unit is plugged in and whether MORF-X can talk to it. It only reads: nothing on your unit or computer is changed.

  1. Plug the unit into this computer by USB and switch it on.
  2. Close HX Edit, POD Go Edit and Line 6 Central if they're open.
  3. Click the button, pick your unit in the list that pops up, and click Connect.

    
    
Then paste them into a message to whoever sent you this link.

  

  
Needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. About MORF-X
